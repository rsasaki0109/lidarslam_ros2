# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

Positive participation includes respectful technical disagreement, empathy,
constructive feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on
the health of the project and its users.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
language or imagery, trolling, personal attacks, publishing another person's
private information, and behavior that would be inappropriate in a
professional engineering community.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity and to anyone officially
representing the project in public or private community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately to `rsasaki0109@gmail.com` with the subject
`[lidarslam_ros2 conduct]`. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
personal information.

The maintainer will protect reporter privacy where possible, review conflicts
of interest, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a
private correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal of content, or a
permanent ban from project spaces.
